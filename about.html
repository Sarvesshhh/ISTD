Our Story

The Indian Society for Training & Development, established in April 1970, is a national level professional & non-profit society registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860. It has a large membership of individuals and institutions involved in the area of training and development of Human Resource from Government, Public and Private Sector Organizations & Enterprises; Educational and Training Institutions and other Professional Bodies. The Society is affiliated to the International Federation of Training and Development Organizations (IFTDO), Geneva. ISTD Organizes Training Programs, all over the country both at Chapter and National Levels.

ISTD operates through various regional chapters and affiliated institutions, which manage local activities and support members across different parts of India. The ISTD Student Chapter SVCE was inaugurated on 23rd February 2023 with the support of Management and guidance of The Principal. The student chapter is committed to nurture the students on technical knowledge, communication, leadership, business acumen and cultural awareness. The club has about 600 active members from various branches and with a strong executive team for coordinating the activities of the club. The student chapter has multiple wings to plan for the events and conduct as per the student’s need.

Our Mission

ISTD is dedicated to improving the quality and effectiveness of training practices throughout India. The society aims to facilitate knowledge sharing among training practitioners and researchers, nurture the development of students and newcomers in the field of training and development, and foster a collaborative environment where expertise and best practices can be exchanged freely.

Our Vision

To nurture students on technical knowledge, communication, leadership, business acumen and cultural awareness — building professional minds equipped for tomorrow's industry. The student chapter strives to be a recognized platform for training, leadership, and professional development at SVCE.
